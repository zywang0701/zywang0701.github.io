Short, plain-language notes on each of my papers — what problem it solves and why it matters.

May 2025 · Multi-source learning

Finding structure every data source agrees on: StablePCA

Pooling datasets and running PCA lets the biggest source dominate. StablePCA instead finds a low-dimensional representation whose worst-case performance across all sources is as good as possible.

Jul 2025 · Domain adaptation

Classifying reliably in a domain you've never labeled: CG-DRO

When you have labels from several source domains but none from the target, which classifier should you trust? One that guards against the worst plausible mixture of sources — with honest confidence intervals.

Dec 2024 · Causal inference

Making invariance-based causal discovery actually scalable

Invariance is a beautiful idea for finding causal predictors, but testing every subset of variables blows up exponentially. We recast the search as a single continuous optimization problem that can be solved efficiently.

2025 · The Annals of Statistics

Learning models that hold up on unseen populations

A model fit to pooled multi-source data quietly optimizes for the majority. We propose distributionally robust learning that performs well for every mixture of the sources — and quantify its uncertainty.

2024 · The R Journal · Software

Confidence intervals in high dimensions, in one R package: SIHR

Lasso-type estimators are biased, so their outputs don't come with valid confidence intervals. SIHR wraps debiased inference for linear and logistic regression into a single, unified interface.
